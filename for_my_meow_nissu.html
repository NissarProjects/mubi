First, I need to say this

I'm sorry, Mubi.

Not to win an argument. Not to make you feel guilty. I want to acknowledge what I did and why it matters.

I know I made mistakes ♡

I kept poking you and repeatedly saying things like “you don't talk to me” or “you don't give me time.” I let my feelings come out in ways that hurt and pressured you.

I should have spoken calmly, listened to you, and respected your space. My feelings are not an excuse to make you feel bad.

I'm truly sorry, Meow. I know words alone cannot fix this. I need to show you change through my actions.

💬

I will communicate better

No more repeated poking or turning my worries into arguments. I'll try to speak calmly and honestly.

🕊️

I will respect your space

I won't demand time, replies, or an instant answer. Your comfort matters too.

👂

I will listen to you

Your feelings matter, even when they're difficult for me to hear. I'll try to understand instead of defend myself.

🌱

I will keep growing

I want to become kinder and more patient—not just for a few days, but as a real change.

Not just promises

Love should feel safe, too.

I can't ask you to trust a promise immediately. Trust comes back slowly when actions match words, consistently.

I want to learn how to be supportive in hard moments, celebrate your good days, and respect you when you need quiet. I know I have to practise that—not just say it here.

A letter for you, Meow ♡

Mubi,

I know things aren't okay between us right now. I understand that my repeated poking and the things I said may have made you feel tired and hurt. I am sorry for my part in that. I should have been more patient, listened better, and respected how you felt.

You mean so much to me, but I understand that loving you also means respecting your feelings and your choices. I don't expect this page to fix everything, and I don't expect an instant reply or forgiveness.

I want to work on myself and become someone who communicates calmly, supports you, and treats you with kindness—even when things are difficult. I know I have to prove that over time.

I care about you, Mubi. If you ever feel ready to talk, I'll listen without turning it into another argument. If you need space, I'll respect that too.

Take care of yourself, Meow. You deserve peace, kindness, and happiness.

Always wishing you well,
Nissu ♡
